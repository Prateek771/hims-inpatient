## Approved product decisions

- One hospital, hospital staff only. No patient portal/accounts or patient mobile alerts. No hospital-size variants or multihospital product work.
- New patient UHID is capital patient initials plus seven random digits, for example Prateek Yadav → PY4827361. Enforce server uniqueness/retry in future implementation. Preserve old UHIDs and separate per-stay IP numbers. Single-name/unidentified-patient edge handling remains to be specified without breaking urgent admission.
- The Doctor decides clinical discharge outside the software. No routine Doctor dashboard/login. The assigned Nurse records the instruction, named clinical authorizer, source/evidence, source time and separate recording identity/time. Nurse does not prescribe or become the clinical authorizer. Retain Doctor identities/history without depending on interactive Doctor sign-in.
- A designated Nurse/ward coordinator per shift maintains Doctor availability; Admin assigns responsibility and backup. Reception can view. Show availability/OT/leave/off-duty and relevant period/expected return, updater and update time. This register is separate from attendance and clinical authorization.
- Nurse publishes the physical patient checklist and coordinates readiness/handover. After Doctor approval, applicable department checks run in parallel; each retains actor, time, status and remarks. Nursing proposes optional applicability; Admin-confirmed N/A needs reason and no relevant service/pending work. Doctor approval, Nursing readiness/exit and final financial authorization remain mandatory.
- Pharmacy records patient medicine charges, accepted unused returns/credits and its own reconciliation clearance. No stock/batch/expiry/procurement or native dispensing/administration product. A return credit is distinct from an actual Cashier refund. Do not introduce an automatic Pharmacy deposit-spending/medicine cash gate from an illustrative transcript example.
- Keep deterministic billing, receipts, deposit allocation, reversals and authorized refunds. Staff verify insurance externally. Insurer authorization/accepted credit is distinct from cash received; accepted insurer responsibility can remain receivable at ordinary exit when patient responsibility and required checks are satisfied. Later ordinary insurer receipts must settle receivables without rewriting exit history.
- Nurse records factual bed vacancy, waiting location and care handover before final room-charge/financial review. Vacancy ends actual room use but keeps the bed held. Admin/Billing owns final financial authorization; Nurse/Admin delivers authorized papers before actual departure; assigned Nurse confirms actual hospital exit. Then closure and authorized cleaning/turnover make the bed Available. Clinical papers remain available after Doctor approval independently of final exit authorization.
- Exclude full Doctor chart/order entry, Nursing MAR/vitals/full care chart and diagnostic production from routine core menus. Preserve all existing data, signed versions, identities and historical financial/clinical evidence. Future removal of workflow dependencies must accompany UI simplification; hiding menus alone will leave old server gates blocking the journey.
- AI remains bounded paperwork assistance, never clinical/coverage/money/clearance authority or autonomous external sending. No new payer/payment/service integrations.
